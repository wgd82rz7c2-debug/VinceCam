# Stage I — 1-minute video elevator pitch script (draft)

> Draft for Duquesne New Venture Challenge Stage I, due 2026-11-15. Timed for ~60
> seconds at a natural, confident speaking pace (~140-150 words). Read it aloud and
> time yourself before recording — cut before adding, if it runs long.

---

**[Hook — 5s]**
Every college business student eventually asks: "Would I actually like this job — or am
I just good at it?"

**[Problem — 12s]**
There's no shortage of career information out there — LinkedIn, Glassdoor, ChatGPT.
What's missing is a way to turn it into one organized decision.

**[Solution — 20s]**
VinceCam is a Career Decision Intelligence platform. We build a living profile of what a
student can actually do, what they enjoy, and where they want their career to go — then
use it to rank careers, compare specific employers and cities, and show exactly what to
do next to close the gap.

**[Differentiation — 12s]**
Most tools stop at "here's your top career match." VinceCam keeps going — through the
employer, the location, the actual internship — and updates itself as real experience
comes in.

**[Close — 8s]**
We're starting with business students right here at Duquesne. VinceCam: what should I
do, where should I do it, and what should I do next?

---

**Word count:** ~155 words (trim ~10-15 if it runs over 60s in practice — the
Differentiation beat is the safest cut).
