VinceCam is a Career Decision Intelligence platform that helps college business students
figure out not just what career to pursue, but where to pursue it and what to do next as
their real experience changes.

Today's students have plenty of career information — LinkedIn, Glassdoor, O*NET,
ChatGPT — but no way to turn it into one organized, personal decision. Existing tools
like CareerExplorer stop at "here are your top career matches." VinceCam starts there
and keeps going: it builds a living profile from a student's resume, stated preferences,
what they actually enjoy doing day to day, and where they want their career to head —
then uses that profile to rank careers, compare specific employers and locations within
a career, and flag realistic next steps to close any readiness gaps. After an internship
or class changes what a student knows about themselves, VinceCam updates the
recommendation and explains exactly why it changed.

We're starting with accounting and finance students, where recruiting is structured,
career paths are well-defined but easy to confuse (is FP&A different from Treasury?
Would I like External Audit more than Transaction Advisory?), and repeated
internship/full-time decisions create natural reasons to return. Free for students;
long-term, we see paid tools for university career centers, which can serve far more
students with structured, persistent decision support than a small advising staff can
offer alone.
