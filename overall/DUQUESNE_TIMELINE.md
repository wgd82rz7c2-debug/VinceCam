## Judging criteria

Management team strength, problem/opportunity, proposed solution, target market,
implementation strategy, business model, financial outlook, exit pathways.

## Eligibility

- **No Duquesne affiliation needed for Stages I or II.**
- **Stage III finalists must include ≥1 Duquesne-affiliated member.** Confirmed
  2026-09-02: already covered — no action needed here.
